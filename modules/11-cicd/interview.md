# Interview Questions: CI/CD

## Beginner questions

1. What problem does ci/cd solve in a platform engineering context?
2. What are the most important components or abstractions to learn first?
3. Why is configuration and environment management important?

## Intermediate questions

4. What are the main failure modes in a ci/cd workflow?
5. How would you debug a production issue under time pressure?
6. What trade-offs exist between simplicity and flexibility?

## Advanced questions

7. How would you design a resilient platform using ci/cd as part of the system?
8. How do you balance governance with developer autonomy?
9. How would you explain the operational cost of a poor platform design?

## Scenario-based questions

10. A deployment fails in a shared environment. What do you do first?
11. A service is slow under load. How do you isolate the bottleneck?
12. A team wants faster delivery with increased security. How do you advise them?
